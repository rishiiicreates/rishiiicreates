## 🌌 About Me

I am **Hrishikesh Yadav (Rishii)**, a B.Tech Computer Science engineering student currently existing between Prayagraj and the SRMIST Delhi-NCR campus. 

My engineering philosophy is simple: build things that actually look good, try to make the backend not fall apart, and never trust a C++ compiler. When I'm not writing code, I'm usually studying for the UPSC exams, designing in Figma, or trying to hit a 74kg physical architecture with decline pushups and a chaotic 4-hour protein window. 

**What I actually do:**
- 💻 **Software & Apps** — Building tools like Housel and DashMetrics
- 🎨 **UI/UX Design** — Wireframing and pretending I'm a graphic designer 
- 🧠 **Algorithms** — Staring at Data Structures until they make sense
- ⚙️ **AI Integration** — Finding ways to make models do the heavy lifting
